About

Life is fascinating. So much we want to understand, so little the universe has to give; but we keep trying.

I'm a computer science and mathematics major drawn to systems and the ideas behind them. Recently, I've been building a compiler for my own language and experimenting with a home Linux server.

I like working close to the fundamentals; understanding how things function from the ground up, from low-level computation to the networks that connect it all. I've also spent time developing games and other projects, and I tend to drift toward problems that make me think a little deeper about how everything fits together.
